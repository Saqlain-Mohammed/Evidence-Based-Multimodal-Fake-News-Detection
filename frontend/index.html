OUR METHODOLOGY

Understand the result.

Content verification starts with asking the right question.

Separate questions, separate models

News credibility, AI authorship, image generation, image manipulation, video deepfakes, and audio spoofing use independent classifiers. One task’s probability cannot answer another task’s question.

Training you can reproduce

Text models learn word and character features directly from public data, with no pretrained weights. The image detector is a randomly initialized convolutional network. Each training run records dataset hashes, splits, class mappings, calibration, and held-out metrics.

Evidence with boundaries

Related reports are retrieved from Google News RSS. Relevance is not agreement. Without a validated entailment model, we do not claim that a headline proves or contradicts your text. A publisher’s name is context, not a credibility percentage.

How to read risk scores

Below 35%: lower model risk. Between 35% and 65%: inconclusive. Above 65%: higher model risk. These are research thresholds, not guarantees that content is authentic or deceptive.

Plagiarism needs a comparison corpus

The text detector measures five-word overlap against a reference you provide. The result is scoped to that reference; it is not an internet-wide originality percentage.

What happens to your data

Inference runs on this server. Analysis reports are saved in its local SQLite database and shared in local mode or restricted to your account when token access is enabled. When enabled, evidence search sends a short text query to Google News. No cloud model API is used.

Available on your devices

The website adapts to phones and can be installed as a web app. The project also includes a browser extension and an Expo mobile client. Native mobile builds and store distribution need their own build environment.
