The city council voted on Tuesday to expand evening bus service after a three-month public consultation. The transport department said the first additional routes would begin next month, subject to a final budget review. The proposal adds two late departures on weekdays and extends service to the hospital on Sundays. A public hearing on the timetable is scheduled before the council publishes its final operating plan.
The council will also review passenger counts after the first six weeks and publish those figures alongside the next quarterly transport report.
